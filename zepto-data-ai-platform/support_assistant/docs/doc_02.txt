Zepto Return and Refund Policy

Zepto accepts returns within 48 hours of delivery for most product categories. Perishable items such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and frozen foods must be reported within 2 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. Non-perishable items in unopened, original packaging can be returned for a full refund. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days and credited to the original payment method. If the original payment was Cash on Delivery (COD), the refund will be issued as Zepto wallet credits. Partial refunds may be issued for items that are damaged or partially consumed. Zepto reserves the right to deny returns if the product has been tampered with or if the return window has expired.
