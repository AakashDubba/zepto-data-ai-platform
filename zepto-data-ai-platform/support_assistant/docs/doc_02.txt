Zepto Return Policy

Zepto accepts returns within 48 hours of delivery for most product categories. Perishable items such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and frozen foods must be reported within 2 hours of delivery with photographic evidence of damage, spoilage, or quality issues. Non-perishable items in unopened, original packaging with all tags intact can be returned within 48 hours. Items cannot be returned if they have been opened, consumed, tampered with, or if the return window has expired. Return requests can be initiated directly through the Zepto app under My Orders by selecting the order and tapping 'Request Return'. Once approved, a pickup agent will collect the item from your delivery address.
