Zepto Gift Cards

Zepto Gift Cards are available in denominations of ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000, and ₹2000 through the Zepto app and website. Gift cards are delivered digitally via email or SMS with a unique 16-digit alphanumeric code and PIN. Gift cards can be redeemed at checkout against any purchase on the Zepto platform and are valid for 12 months from the date of issuance. Unused balances remain available on the card or can be merged into your Zepto wallet balance. Gift cards cannot be reloaded, transferred for cash, or used to purchase additional gift cards. For corporate or bulk gift card inquiries (orders of 50+ cards), businesses can contact business@zepto.co for special corporate pricing and custom branding options.
