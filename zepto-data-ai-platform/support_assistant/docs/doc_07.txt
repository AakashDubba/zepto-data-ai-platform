Zepto Customer Support Hours and Channels

Zepto customer support is available through multiple channels. In-app live chat is available 24/7 with average response times of under 5 minutes. Phone support is available from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST, seven days a week, at 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free). Email support at help@zepto.co has a response time of 4–6 hours during business hours. For urgent issues related to ongoing deliveries, the in-app chat is the fastest channel with priority routing for active orders. Social media support is available on Twitter (@ZeptoNow) and Instagram (@ZeptoIndia) from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST. Zepto Pass members receive priority support with dedicated agents and average response times under 2 minutes across all channels.
