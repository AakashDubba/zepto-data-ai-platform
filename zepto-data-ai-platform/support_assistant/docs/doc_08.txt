Zepto Payment Methods and Security

Zepto accepts a wide range of payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking from all major Indian banks, Zepto wallet credits, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders up to ₹5000. All digital payments are processed through PCI DSS-compliant payment gateways with 256-bit SSL encryption. Two-factor authentication is required for all saved card transactions. In case of payment failures, the amount is automatically refunded to the source within 5–7 business days. Zepto does not store your complete card details on its servers. For COD orders, exact change is appreciated as delivery riders carry limited change. Zepto wallet can be topped up in multiples of ₹100 and has a maximum balance limit of ₹10,000.
