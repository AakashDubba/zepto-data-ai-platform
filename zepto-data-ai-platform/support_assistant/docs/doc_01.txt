Zepto Delivery Policy

Zepto offers ultra-fast delivery within 10 minutes for most locations in metro cities. Delivery is available from 6:00 AM to midnight, seven days a week. Standard delivery is free for orders above ₹99. For orders below ₹99, a delivery fee of ₹25 is charged. Express delivery (guaranteed under 8 minutes) is available for a premium of ₹15 on top of any existing delivery charges. Delivery times may vary during peak hours (12 PM – 2 PM and 7 PM – 10 PM) or adverse weather conditions. If your delivery is delayed beyond the promised time, you are eligible for Zepto credits worth ₹20 applied to your next order.
