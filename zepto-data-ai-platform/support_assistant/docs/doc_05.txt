Zepto Order Tracking

Once an order is confirmed, customers can track its status in real time via the live tracking map in the Zepto app. The five distinct order stages are: Order Placed, Order Confirmed, Being Packed, Out for Delivery, and Delivered. Push notifications are sent automatically at each stage transition. The app displays real-time GPS location updates of the delivery partner once the order is Out for Delivery, along with an estimated arrival countdown. If an order remains in 'Out for Delivery' status for more than 15 minutes, the app automatically alerts the local dark-store manager, and the customer is connected to priority support. Contactless delivery options and digital delivery confirmations with photo proof are provided for every completed order.
