Zepto Gift Cards

Zepto Gift Cards are available in denominations of ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000, and ₹2000. Gift cards can be purchased through the app and sent via email or SMS to recipients. Each gift card has a unique 16-digit code that can be redeemed at checkout. Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and cannot be extended beyond the expiry date. Multiple gift cards can be combined in a single order. Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards and are non-refundable once purchased. The remaining balance on a partially used gift card is automatically saved to the recipient's Zepto wallet. Corporate bulk gift card orders (50+ cards) are available at a 5% discount by contacting business@zepto.co.
