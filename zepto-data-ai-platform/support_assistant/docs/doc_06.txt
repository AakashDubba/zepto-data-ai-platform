Zepto Order Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 60 seconds of order placement directly through the app by clicking 'Cancel Order'. Once an order transitions to the 'Being Packed' status after the 60-second window, a nominal cancellation fee of ₹25 applies to cover packaging and inventory handling costs. Orders that have reached 'Out for Delivery' status cannot be cancelled via the app; customers must refuse delivery at the doorstep. For doorstep refusals, the order amount minus a ₹50 reverse-logistics fee will be refunded to the original payment method. Recurring subscription orders can be cancelled free of charge up to 12 hours prior to the scheduled delivery slot. All cancellation refunds follow standard refund timelines.
