Zepto Pass Membership Program

Zepto Pass is our premium membership program available at ₹149/month or ₹999/year. Members enjoy unlimited free deliveries on orders above ₹49, priority customer support with average response times under 2 minutes, and exclusive member-only discounts of up to 15% on select categories. Zepto Pass members also get early access to flash sales and seasonal promotions. The membership auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Members can cancel anytime through the app under Settings → Manage Subscription. Upon cancellation, benefits remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. Zepto Pass is non-transferable and limited to one membership per account.
