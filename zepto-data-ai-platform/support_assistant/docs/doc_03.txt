Zepto Refund Policy

Refunds are processed promptly upon return approval or order cancellation. For digital payments (UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking), refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank's processing timelines. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are issued immediately as Zepto wallet credits, which can be used on future purchases or transferred to your linked bank account. In cases of missing items or delivered damaged goods, instant refunds to Zepto wallet credits are issued upon verification. If a refund is not credited within 7 business days, customers can contact support at help@zepto.co with their order and transaction reference IDs.
