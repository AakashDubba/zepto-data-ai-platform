Zepto Pass Membership Program

Zepto Pass is our premium customer membership program available at ₹149 per month or ₹999 per year. Members enjoy unlimited free delivery on all orders above ₹49, priority customer support with response times under 2 minutes, and exclusive member-only discounts of up to 15% across select grocery and personal care categories. Zepto Pass members also receive early access to flash sales, exclusive seasonal promotions, and free surprise samples with select orders. The membership auto-renews at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to renewal. Members can manage or cancel their subscription at any time via the app under Account Settings → Zepto Pass. Benefits remain active until the end of the current paid period.
